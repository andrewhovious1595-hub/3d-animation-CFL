EVIDENTIARY PILLAR 01

Federal Rule of Evidence 702 & Daubert Criteria

Under the amended FRE Rule 702, the proponent must demonstrate by a preponderance of the evidence that expert visual testimony is based on sufficient facts or data and reliable principles applied reliably to the facts.

Daubert / FRE 702 Factor ClearFault Legal Technical Execution
1. Testability & Falsifiability All spatial coordinates, camera focal lengths, and vehicle velocity vectors are exported in open CAD/CSV logs so opposing experts can independently verify every measurement.
2. Known or Potential Error Rate Terrestrial LiDAR scans carry a certified ±2.0mm manufacturer accuracy tolerance; photogrammetric residuals are logged in sub-pixel RMS error reports.
3. Peer Review & Published Physics Vehicular momentum, crush stiffness (WinSMASH/EDSMAC), and photometric lighting calculations follow SAE, IESNA, and ASTM peer-reviewed standards.
4. Substantive vs. Illustrative Distinction We separate Illustrative Demonstratives (Rule 611(a)) from Substantive Scientific Reconstructions (Rule 702) so counsel can lay the exact foundation required by the trial judge.
