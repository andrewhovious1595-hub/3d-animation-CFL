04 // COURTROOM FOUNDATION & FAQ

Evidentiary Admissibility & Technical Protocol FAQ

How does ClearFault Legal authenticate 3D models for court admission?
Every model is accompanied by a Technical Methodology Affidavit documenting raw input sources (E57/RCP LiDAR files, DICOM headers, Bosch CDR reports, CAD schematics), software version logs, camera focal-length calibration data, and SHA-256 cryptographic hashes proving zero post-capture tampering.
Do you work with both plaintiff and defense counsel, as well as pro se individuals?
Yes. As an objective forensic engineering and spatial rendering studio, we serve plaintiff trial firms, insurance defense counsel, Special Investigations Units (SIU), criminal defense teams, and self-represented individuals needing clear visual proof of physical facts.
What raw evidence files can we send to initiate a 3D reconstruction?
We ingest laser scan point clouds (.E57, .LAS, .RCP), drone photogrammetry sets, traffic collision reports, dashcam/CCTV video files, vehicle EDR black-box exports, medical CT/MRI DICOM folders, architectural PDFs/DWGs, and site photographs.
